ABOUT MARY G.

Mary G. is a traditional artist and professional art instructor dedicated to exploring diverse artistic styles and techniques. Her work spans a wide spectrum, including hyper-realism, cubism, surrealism, and abstract art, utilizing mediums such as oil, acrylic, charcoal, and graphite.

Driven by a passion for visual storytelling, her creations range from intricately detailed realism to expressive structural forms, constantly pushing the boundaries of traditional boundaries to capture the essence of her subjects.
